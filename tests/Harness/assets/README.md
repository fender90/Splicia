# Test photos

Public-domain NASA crew portraits from Wikimedia Commons, used to make the face test videos (see `MakeVideos` in
`Main.cs`). They are 1920-pixel-wide renditions of:

- `apollo11-crew.jpg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apollo_11_Crew.jpg (NASA, public domain)
- `expedition64-crew.jpg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Expedition_64_crew_portrait.jpg (NASA/Cindy Bush, public domain)

Hard cases for finding faces (turned away, in profile, looking down, partly covered), 1280-pixel-wide renditions from
the NASA Image and Video Library:

- `worker-gaiter.jpg`: a man bending over, turned to the side, a neck gaiter over his nose and a cap; https://images.nasa.gov/details/MAF_20200519_SLS%20back%20to%20work-322 (NASA, public domain)
- `worker-turned-away.jpg`: a man in a mask seen from behind, his face turned up and away; https://images.nasa.gov/details/MAF_20200519_SLS%20back%20to%20work-197 (NASA, public domain)
- `eye-exam.jpg`: one astronaut looking down over another, whose face is under his hands; https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss034e035949 (NASA, public domain)
- `interview.jpg`: two men talking, one seen from behind in near profile; https://images.nasa.gov/details/KSC-04pd0306 (NASA, public domain)
- `station-meal.jpg`: an astronaut in profile looking down, another further back; https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss043e086137 (NASA, public domain)
